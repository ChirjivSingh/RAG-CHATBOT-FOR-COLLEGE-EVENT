2. **Identification:**

## 6. Project Submission and Deliverables

- **Submission Deadline:** Sunday, April 5, 2026, at **11:00 AM IST sharp**.
- **Late Submission Policy:**
  - A 5-minute grace period exists between 11:00 AM and 11:05 AM IST for network latency. Any submission received in this 5-minute window incurs a mandatory **10% penalty** on the final aggregate score.
  - At **11:05 AM IST**, the submission portal locks permanently. No submissions via email, Slack, Discord, or USB drives will be accepted under any circumstances.
- **Mandatory Submission Requirements:**
  1. **GitHub/GitLab Repository Link:** Must be public, contain an open-source license (MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL, etc.), and show clear commit logs during the 24-hour hackathon window.
  2. **README.md:** Must include project name, problem statement, architecture diagram, setup instructions, tech stack list, and team credentials.
  3. **Video Demonstration Link:** A 2 to 3-minute video (hosted on YouTube/Loom/Google Drive) showing live software functionality.
  4. **Live Deployment / Executable Demo (Optional but recommended):** Hosted URL (Vercel, Render, AWS, Streamlit Cloud, etc.) or APK.
- **Pitch Duration:**
  - Preliminary round: 4 minutes presentation + 3 minutes judge Q&A.
  - Final round (Top 10): 5 minutes pitch + 3 minutes live Q&A.

## 7. Code Originality, AI Tools, and Ethics

1. **Originality and Prior Work:**
   - All core project codebase and intellectual artifacts must be built from scratch during the hackathon window (starting 10:30 AM IST on April 4, 2026).
   - Use of open-source libraries, UI packages, public APIs, and boilerplate scaffolding (e.g., `create-react-app`, Next.js starters, Django skeleton) is allowed provided they are declared in the README.
   - Presenting pre-existing commercial projects, past college capstone projects, or previously submitted competition entries will result in immediate disqualification.
2. **AI Tool Usage Policy:**
   - AI coding assistants (such as GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor) are **permitted for assistance** (e.g., generating boilerplate, fixing syntax errors, or exploring API documentation).
   - Entirely generating project solutions without human architectural contribution is prohibited.
   - During judging, any team member may be asked to explain any block of code line-by-line. If a team cannot explain their own code, that code will be discarded from scoring.
3. **Plagiarism and Integrity:**
   - Plagiarism screening tools will analyze submitted repositories.
   - Copying code from another participating team or cloning existing repositories without attribution leads to immediate disqualification and reporting to respective college authorities.

## 8. Judging Criteria and Scoring Breakdown

All judging evaluations are objective and calculated out of **100 Total Percentage Points**:

| Criterion | Weight (%) | Description |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Problem Solving & Innovation** | **25%** | Novelty of approach, clarity of problem formulation, and originality of solution. |
| **Technical Complexity & Implementation** | **25%** | Architecture quality, code cleanliness, depth of engineering, and working functionality. |
| **Feasibility & Practical Usability** | **20%** | Real-world viability, operational feasibility, edge-case resilience, and user impact. |
| **UI/UX & Design Quality** | **15%** | User interface aesthetics, accessibility, seamless user journeys, and responsiveness. |
| **Presentation & Q&A Response** | **15%** | Clarity of pitch, demonstration effectiveness, and quality of answers to judges' queries. |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **The weights sum to exactly 100%.** |

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### Q1: Can students from different IPU colleges form a team together?
**Yes.** Inter-college teams are completely allowed, as long as every team member is an active, enrolled student of an institute or college affiliated with GGSIPU.

#### Q2: Is there any registration or entry fee?
**No.** Registration and participation in InnovateX 2026 are 100% free of charge.

#### Q3: Can I participate individually if I cannot find a team?
**No.** The rulebook mandates teams of 2 to 4 members. Solo entries will not be registered or reviewed.

#### Q4: Can a team change its members after registration?
**Yes, but only until March 20, 2026, at 5:00 PM IST.** Send an email to `roster@innovatex.techspace.org` with details. No changes are permitted after check-in on April 4.

#### Q5: What if our project is 2 minutes late in submission?
Submissions between 11:00 AM and 11:05 AM IST on April 5 are accepted under a **10% deduction penalty** on the total score. After 11:05 AM IST, the portal locks and submissions are strictly rejected.

#### Q6: Are we allowed to use pre-built APIs or UI libraries?
**Yes.** Open-source UI packages (Tailwind, Bootstrap, Material UI), public libraries, and standard APIs are encouraged. However, core application code and logic must be written during the hackathon.

#### Q7: Can we use Generative AI tools like ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot?
**Yes, for assistance and boilerplate code.** However, teams must be able to explain all submitted code during judging. Unexplained AI-generated code will not receive points.

#### Q8: What if one team member cannot attend in person on the event day?
As long as at least 2 members check in physically at the USICT campus, the team remains eligible. If attendance drops below 2 members, the remaining student cannot compete alone.

#### Q9: Will overnight stay arrangements be provided at USICT?
**Yes.** Separate resting rooms for male and female participants with mattresses, blankets, and 24/7 security are arranged inside USICT E-Block.

#### Q10: What are the exact judging criteria weights?
Problem Solving & Innovation (25%), Technical Complexity & Implementation (25%), Feasibility & Practical Usability (20%), UI/UX & Design Quality (15%), and Presentation & Q&A Response (15%), totaling exactly 100%.

#### Q11: How are prize ties resolved?
First by the score in **Technical Complexity & Implementation**. If still tied, the score in **Problem Solving & Innovation** is used.

#### Q12: Are food and refreshments provided for free?
**Yes.** Breakfast, lunch, dinner, midnight snacks, and coffee rounds are provided free of cost to all checked-in participants.

#### Q13: Can non-IPU students participate?
**No.** InnovateX 2026 is an inter-IPU hackathon open exclusively to students of GGSIPU main campus and affiliated institutes. Non-IPU colleges are ineligible for this edition.

#### Q14: What hardware should participants bring?
Participants must bring their own personal laptops, chargers, and any specialized peripherals needed for their project. TechSpace provides power outlets, Wi-Fi, and basic display cables.

#### Q15: Will participants receive attendance / duty leave certificates?
**Yes.** TechSpace and USICT will issue official on-duty event participation certificates that can be submitted to your college department for attendance consideration.

#### Q16: Can a team submit more than one project?
**No.** Each team may submit only one project under one primary track.
